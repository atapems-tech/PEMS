## Evidence

Urgent fixes for one customer tend to create forks. The policy is to fix on the release line, backport and ship a patch release.

## Prompt

Define the flow: reproduce on the customer's version, fix on the release branch, tag a patch, ship through the normal updater, record in the registry.

## Requirement

A rehearsed hotfix goes from report to delivered patch and registry entry within the target time.
