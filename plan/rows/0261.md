## Evidence

Unsigned installers trigger OS warnings and cannot be trusted by customers. The update-signing key must be distinct from the licence-signing key.

## Prompt

Obtain a code-signing certificate, sign binaries and installers with timestamps, create an update-package signing key, and document custody next to L_KEYS.

## Requirement

Installers and updaters verify on a clean Windows machine; a tampered package is rejected by the updater.
