## Evidence

Owner decision 2026-10-15: 'times' means a time period. No count-based (activation/launch) licences. Lifetime and term cover sales; trial and demo cover pre-sales.

## Prompt

Each type has its own validity logic: term = start and end date checked against a tamper-guarded clock (L_CLOCK); lifetime = no expiry; warning before expiry for term licences.

## Requirement

Boundary tests: expiry at Jalali midnight, renewal before/after expiry, lifetime shows no expiry warning, a trial cannot be extended without a new licence.
