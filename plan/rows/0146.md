## Evidence

A public demo is an abuse target.

## Prompt

A list of disabled actions, a separate scanned upload folder, email redirected to a sink, and strict separation from the main domain: host-only cookies on the demo subdomain, no shared sessions, separate CSP/CORS, no link into any landing back end.

## Requirement

A light penetration test finds nothing critical, and nothing obtained on the demo subdomain gives access to the landing website or its pipeline.
