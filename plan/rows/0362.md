## Evidence

Choosing sensible min and max values blind is error-prone; the real data of the field is the best suggestion. Advisory hints must never block a save.

## Prompt

1) Endpoint returning count, min, max, average, standard deviation and empty rate for one numeric field, filtered by the central row-level security (#178) and cached. 2) Form designer shows the figures next to the range inputs and offers 'use observed range'. 3) The form shows a non-blocking 'unusual value' hint when a value is far from the average in units of the standard deviation. 4) Setting to turn hints off per field.

## Requirement

Done when: the designer shows live statistics and can fill the range; an unusual value shows a hint but saves; a user cannot see statistics of records they cannot access.
