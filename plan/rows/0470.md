## Evidence

Gap analysis SCRUM-427 (docs/procurement/PROCUREMENT_STANDARDS_GAP_ANALYSIS.md, section 6 item 1, gaps 16, 17, 29): PurchaseOrder is a header only (no lines, no link to MR, tender or award); a manual PO goes Draft -> Issued with no approval or authority by amount; imports create Issued. Owner approved batch 1 (#470 + #471) on 2026-10-29.

## Prompt

See the Jira issue SCRUM-470. PO lines (quantity, unit, unit price, required date, registered part) linked to MR item, tender and winning bid; header value = sum of lines. Draft -> Submitted -> Approved -> Issued on the workflow engine, authority limits by amount by generalising ContractApprovalAuthorityRule (no copy), creator != approver, limits configured by the admin. Imports stay Issued. Migration only, audit, permissions. Owner decisions 2026-10-29: the PO comes only through the pre-procurement path of #471 (MR -> tender/award -> PO); a separate PO authority table (levels, amount ranges, currency, responsibility) with an admin page, nothing hard-coded; free-text lines as SAP text items / IFS no-part lines: stock lines catalogued only, one-time/direct-use/service lines free text with description, unit, commodity group and reason, a setting can force catalogued-only.

## Requirement

Done when: a PO built from an MR has lines that trace back to the MR items; a stock line cannot be free text; it cannot be issued before approval at the right authority level; a creator cannot approve their own PO; imports still work; tests and browser QA pass.
