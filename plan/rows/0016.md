## Evidence

A serial that is sequential but not unique in the DB is a known defect pattern. Engineering documents and work orders need traceable numbers.

## Prompt

Patterns such as WO-{YYYY}-{#####}; atomic allocation with DB locking and a unique index.

## Requirement

A 50-parallel-request test yields no duplicate numbers; the pattern is editable and versioned.

## Report
