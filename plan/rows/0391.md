## Evidence

Found 2026-10-27 by the data-entry check of SCRUM-416 (PEMS.UI.Tests DataEntryUnaffectedTests): a number input posts 12.5, the fa culture decimal separator is U+066B, so the binder rejects 12.5 while 12 binds; typed Persian digits are rejected. Dates are safe because PEMS.Web keeps the Gregorian calendar on fa (Program.cs).

## Prompt

One rule for numeric and date input binding: type=number and type=date bind with the invariant culture, and Persian or Arabic-Indic digits and U+066B are normalised to Latin digits and a dot before parsing; implement where the field components live (#322) and apply it to PEMS.Web through one binder. SCRUM-421.
