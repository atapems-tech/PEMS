## Evidence

Lets you sell a lifetime licence with an annual maintenance period that controls which updates a customer may install.

## Prompt

Add the updates-until field to the licence (L_LICFMT), per module where modules are sold separately, and the build date to the manifest; the updater compares them; renewal extends the date.

## Requirement

A customer with expired maintenance keeps running but cannot install newer packages until renewed.
