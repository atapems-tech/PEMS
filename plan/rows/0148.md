## Evidence

A demo without lead capture loses sales. The request form lives in the landing website (pems-landing repository) while the demo lives on the subdomain, so the two only exchange a link and the lead record.

## Prompt

A short form on the landing site with privacy consent that posts to the CRM and notifies the sales team, and gives the visitor the demo address. No shared session or database between landing and demo.

## Requirement

Every request is in the CRM with its source.
