## Evidence

The scenario the owner described: a full machine image brought up elsewhere. Different hardware fails the fingerprint and machine-bound seal; restoring on the same hardware or an identical VM needs a monotonic counter and lifecycle trail.

## Prompt

Monotonic counter in the seal plus a hash chain of the audit log; each start raises the counter; a counter lower than recorded means suspected restore; log the event and require re-activation.

## Requirement

Documented test scenarios: (1) image on different hardware: rejected; (2) cloned VM: detected through the virtual TPM/hypervisor identifier where available, otherwise by the restore-detection counter, with the EULA clause as backstop; (3) restore from an old snapshot: warning and re-activation.
