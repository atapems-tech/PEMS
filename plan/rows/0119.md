## Prompt

A package content control script. Extend the scan to everything handed to a customer or auditor (package, docs, sources): internal IP addresses, host names and SSH or service account names (audit finding S-6: 192.168.1.x addresses and an SSH account in deploy/caddy/Caddyfile, docs/audits/* and docs/deployment/*), and run a secrets scan (gitleaks, MIT licence) over the delivery.

## Requirement
