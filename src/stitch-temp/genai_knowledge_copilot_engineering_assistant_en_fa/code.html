SESSION: TR-900 DIAGNOSTICS

I'm seeing abnormal vibration levels (peak 8.5 mm/s) on the TR-900 forward bearing during start-up sequence. It stabilizes after reaching 3000 RPM. What could be the root cause based on our historical data?
در حین راه‌اندازی TR-900، لرزش غیرعادی روی یاتاقان جلویی (پیک 8.5 mm/s) مشاهده می‌کنم. پس از رسیدن به 3000 دور در دقیقه تثبیت می‌شود. علت ریشه‌ای بر اساس داده‌های تاریخی ما چیست؟

smart_toy

Based on the TR-900 technical manuals and historical work orders, a transient high vibration peak during run-up that stabilizes at operating speed often indicates a temporary thermal bow or a slight rub condition before thermal equilibrium is reached.

menu_book Excerpt: TR-900 Maintenance Manual (Section 4.2)

"Vibration excursions exceeding 7.0 mm/s during the critical speed transition (2200-2600 RPM) that resolve upon reaching nominal speed (3000 RPM) are typically indicative of rotor thermal bowing. Verify adequate barring gear operation duration prior to start."

5 Whys Analysis Starter

  • Why is the vibration high at start-up? Rotor may have a temporary thermal bow.
  • Why is there a thermal bow? Uneven cooling during the last shutdown.
  • Why was cooling uneven? The barring gear might not have operated for the specified duration.
